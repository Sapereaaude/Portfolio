A monthly pulse on 3D tools

Are bike designers, engineers and 3D modellers happy with their tools, month after month?

Role
Researcher
When
2025
With

Phase

The context

Bike designers, engineers and 3D modellers depend on 3D tools daily. Their frustrations surfaced in hallways, never in numbers.

02

The question

  • Measure satisfaction with 3D tools over time
  • Turn feedback into decided fixes, every month
  • Keep the effort tiny for respondents
03

What I did

  • Monthly survey with a one-week response window
  • One week of analysis
  • A one-hour action workshop with the team
  • Tool comparison: Medallia, Airtable, Google Forms
