Side project · prototype · 2026

Cassandre

Test what you can. Research what you can’t.

Role
Concept, research, prototype
When
2026
Stage
Prototype, not a validated product
For
Developers and product teams
01

The context

Why spend human intelligence on problems we already know how to identify? Cassandre is where that question ended up, after years of writing methods down, teaching them, then turning them into tools and assistants.

02

The question

  • Which UX issues are predictable enough to be caught before involving human participants?
  • What should stay with human research, whatever the tooling?
03

What it does

A UX pretesting tool. It aims to identify predictable UX issues before involving human participants, using:

  • Established HCI knowledge
  • Accessibility criteria
  • Heuristics
  • Verifiable interaction checks

The goal is not to replace human research. It is to reserve it for the questions that need human understanding: needs, motivations, mental models, product value, real-world behaviour and changing contexts.

04

What it is not

  • Automated checks can verify certain conditions. That is all they verify.
  • Other findings are presented as evidence-based UX risks or hypotheses.
  • It does not claim to simulate real human behaviour.
  • It is a prototype, not a validated commercial product.
05

What remains unknown

How well it works. There are no performance metrics or results to show yet, and I won’t invent any.

To complete as the prototype gets tested.
