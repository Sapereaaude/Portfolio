Cassandre

Test what you can. Research what you can’t.

A UX pretesting tool for developers and product teams. It aims to catch predictable UX issues before involving human participants, using established HCI knowledge, accessibility criteria, heuristics and verifiable interaction checks.

The goal is not to replace human research. It is to keep it for the questions that need a human: needs, motivations, mental models, product value, real-world behaviour.
