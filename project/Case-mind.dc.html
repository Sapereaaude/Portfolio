Alan Mind

How can an app help destigmatise therapy?

Role
Senior UXR
When
2021–22
With
Alan Mind team
Phase
User research & ResearchOps
01

The context

Therapy was still stigmatised, even taboo, in France. We wanted to map how French people think about therapy, and whether an app could lower the friction.

02

The question

  • Understand the French population’s mental models of therapy
  • Find what stops people from seeking help
  • Find where Alan Mind can fit into that journey
03

What I did

  • Interviews on beliefs about, and experiences of, therapy
  • Mental-model mapping
  • Concept and wording tests
04

What we learned

  • People needed therapy, and wanted it.
  • They wanted it on desktop, not on mobile.
  • Some were wary of therapy paid for by their employer: would it come with strings attached?
  • The main friction was finding a good therapist.
05

What changed

  • We quickly moved to desktop and dropped mobile.
  • We added an orientation session, to help people find the right therapist.
06

What I liked

Having an impact on people’s lives.

07

What I’d change

More time, and more psychological training, to absorb what came up in the interviews.
